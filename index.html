The Unsolved Vault is a YouTube channel that explores real unsolved cases from around the world: strange disappearances, unexplained deaths, and stories that still have no answer.

Our videos are researched from public sources and narrated with an AI-generated voice. Images come from Wikimedia Commons and other free-license sources, credited in each video description.
