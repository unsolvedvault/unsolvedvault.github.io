Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

This policy describes how "Unsolved Vault Uploader" (the "App"), operated by The Unsolved Vault, handles information when it uses Google and YouTube services.

Who uses the App

The App is a private tool used only by the owner of The Unsolved Vault YouTube channel to upload and manage that channel's own videos. It is not offered to the public and does not have other users.

What data the App accesses

With the channel owner's permission (Google OAuth), the App uses the YouTube Data API to:

  • read the basic information of the authorized channel (its name and ID), to confirm it is uploading to the correct channel;
  • upload videos, thumbnails, titles, descriptions and tags to that channel, and set their publishing schedule;
  • update the title, description and tags of videos it has uploaded.

The App does not access viewers' data, comments, subscribers' personal information, payment information, or any other Google account data.

How data is stored

The authorization token is stored only on the channel owner's personal computer. No data is sent to, stored on, or shared with any server operated by The Unsolved Vault or any third party, other than Google/YouTube itself as needed to upload the videos.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share any user data with anyone.

Google API Services

The App's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The App uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account → Third-party apps & services. Deleting the token file from the computer also removes the App's access.
